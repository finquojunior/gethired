Read-only review of `main` at 28f4689: what a new recruiter and a first-time candidate hit without the builder next to them, plus code that can go. Severity: **High** sends the wrong email, loses work, or dead-ends the user; **Med** confusing, undiscoverable, or silently no-op; **Low** polish, consistency, accessibility.

Three patterns explain most of the staff-side findings. First, the shared submit button shows its success toast whenever the form stops being busy, so an action that quietly returned early still reports "Moved to stage, candidate emailed". Second, there is not a single confirmation dialog in the app, yet several one-click actions send email to candidates. Third, the role model described on the Team page (interviewers see only their openings) is not what the code enforces: everyone sees everything, and non-staff roles crash into the generic error page on most buttons.

On the candidate side the recurring gap is orientation: no company name on pages or emails, no pipeline progress indicator, a bare Next.js 404 for closed roles and expired links, and no way to recover a lost portal link. The codebase itself is clean of dead functions; what remains is duplicated helpers, five point-in-time review documents whose findings have all shipped, and a few config leftovers.

# Staff app

## Feedback, errors, destructive actions

### S1 · High · Success toasts fire on failure and no-op paths
The shared submit button toasts whenever pending flips back to false, so it cannot tell success from an early return. Bulk bar with zero rows ticked shows "Moved to stage, candidate emailed". A lost booking race shows "Done". Creating zero slots shows "Done". Every unnamed form shows "Done".
**Fix:** Return `{ ok, message }` from actions through `useActionState`, or at minimum drop the default message and disable bulk buttons until a checkbox is ticked with a live "N selected" count.
_components/SubmitButton.tsx:27 · app/app/candidates/actions.ts:111, 351 · app/app/openings/actions.ts:466 · openings/[id]/applications/page.tsx:248_

### S2 · High · No confirmation on irreversible, email-sending bulk actions
"Reject + email now", "Mark hired" and "Move to stage" on N selected candidates fire instantly. Select-all ticks the whole visible list. The count only appears after submit.
**Fix:** Live "N selected" next to the bar; confirm for reject and hire always, and for move when N is greater than one.
_openings/[id]/applications/page.tsx:240 · components/SelectAll.tsx · components/BulkProgress.tsx:10_

### S3 · High · Board drag-and-drop moves and emails the candidate with no confirm, no toast, no undo
